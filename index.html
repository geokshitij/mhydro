The Mountain Hydrology Group is an independent research collective of scientists and engineers working across universities, research institutes, industry, and government on four continents. We study how water shapes, and threatens, life in mountain regions. Our work spans floods and flash floods, landslides and debris flows, glacial lakes and their outburst floods, groundwater, wildfire–hydrology interactions, and the resilience of communities and infrastructure.

We combine process-based understanding with artificial intelligence and remote sensing to advance hazard and water science in data-scarce mountain environments. We also mentor young scientists and support the next generation of mountain researchers.

Nitesh Khadka at Imja Tsho glacial lake in the Everest region.
