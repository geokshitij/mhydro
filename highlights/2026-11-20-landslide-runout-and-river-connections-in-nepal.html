--- layout: highlight title: "Landslide runout and river connections in Nepal" date: 2026-11-20 description: "Schmitt, Shikshita Bhandari, Vogl & Marc, Natural Hazards and Earth System Sciences (2026). Co-led by group member Shikshita Bhandari (Stanford University). A new framework maps about one million potential landslides in the Kaligandaki Basin, Nepal, and traces where each would run out. Runout enlarges the hazard area by about half, and a third of the landslides connect to rivers." share_image: /img/highlights/nhess-2026-paper-thumb.jpg ---
